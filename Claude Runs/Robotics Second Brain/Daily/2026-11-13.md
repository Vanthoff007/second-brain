## Worth your time

### StrataSLAM: open-source multi-floor 3D SLAM for ROS 2
- Summary: From the Intelligent Robotics Lab (Universidad Rey Juan Carlos). It keeps one submap per floor and builds a global map from them. It detects and classifies floor transitions (stairs, ramps, elevators, flights between floors) and measures elevator rides with the IMU "even when the camera or LiDAR only sees the cabin". A floor gate means "no loop closure is ever accepted between two floors", so identical floors are never merged. [source fact, author post]
- Why you: false loop closures between look-alike places and losing height when the sensor moves with its surroundings are the same failure modes a VIO/SLAM stack hits in multi-storey indoor UAV flights.
- Limitations: no accuracy, runtime or compute figures in the email. The sensor set, backend, licence and repo URL were not visible (truncated preview). It is built for ground robots and floor transitions; how it handles a flying platform is unknown.
- Next action: open the thread, get the repo, and check whether the floor-gate logic can be separated from the LiDAR backend.
- Link: https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/strataslam-open-source-multi-floor-slam-for-ros-2-stairs-elevators-ramps-identical-floors/59649

### ZED ROS 2 Wrapper 5.6: CUDA zero-copy with rosidl::Buffer (ROS 2 Lyrical, Isaac ROS 5)
- Summary: New wrapper release that adds CUDA zero-copy image transport using `rosidl::Buffer`, targeting ROS 2 Lyrical and Isaac ROS 5. [source fact, title only]
- Why you: zero-copy GPU image transport is the main lever on camera-to-inference latency and CPU load in an embedded perception pipeline.
- Limitations: only the title was available. There are no latency or CPU numbers, and no Jetson model. It is Stereolabs/NVIDIA-specific and does not carry over directly to RB5 or Raspberry Pi 5.
- Next action: read the post for measured numbers before deciding whether the `rosidl::Buffer` pattern is worth copying in your own camera drivers.
- Link: https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/zed-ros-2-wrapper-5-6-cuda-zero-copy-with-rosidl-buffer-for-ros-2-lyrical-and-isaac-ros-5/59608

### Passively morphing quadrotor for contact-mediated narrow-gap traversal
- Summary: A drone that passively changes shape on contact. The prototype "passively reduces its width from 31.3 to 14.1 cm and traverses a 15 cm wide opening at a 2 m/s reference speed", and is controlled under deflection with NMPC. [measured, quoted by Weekly Robotics #384]
- Why you: UAV autonomy in confined spaces. Contact-tolerant airframes change what the perception and planning stack must guarantee about clearance.
- Limitations: newsletter summary only. Paper title, authors and URL were not resolved (tracked redirect). There are no state-estimation details, and it is mechanical/control work rather than perception.
- Next action: low priority. Find the paper by its title if you work on narrow-gap flight.
- Link: Weekly Robotics #384 (2026-11-10, item "Task-Driven Closed-Loop Design Optimization of Passively Morphing Quadrotors…"); paper URL not resolved.

## Research opportunity
**Untested hypothesis:** a floor/level gate (in the spirit of StrataSLAM's) driven by barometer + IMU vertical-motion detection cuts false loop closures in VIO/SLAM on UAVs that fly through multi-storey buildings with repetitive floors.
- Question: does gating loop-closure candidates by an estimated level index cut false-positive closures without losing true ones?
- Method: estimate a discrete level from barometer + IMU-integrated vertical displacement, with hysteresis. Reject place-recognition candidates whose level differs. Compare against an ungated baseline (e.g. VINS-Fusion loop closure).
- Hardware/software: a UAV or handheld rig with camera, IMU and barometer; an existing VIO + loop-closure stack; a multi-floor building with similar floors.
- Feasibility: moderate. Gating is a thin filter in front of an existing loop-closure module. The risks are barometer drift from HVAC or door pressure changes, and open atria or mezzanines where "floor" is not well defined.
- Metrics: false-positive and true-positive loop-closure counts, ATE RMSE with and without the gate, and how often the level index is wrong.
