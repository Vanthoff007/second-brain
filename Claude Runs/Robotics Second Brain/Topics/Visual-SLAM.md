## Updates — 2026-11-09
- **Reflection-robust stereo VIO (2611.05938, [measured] by authors):** virtual features from specular floors are triangulated below the ground plane. A scalar Kalman filter with NIS gating (gate 10, 1 DoF) estimates ground height; only features below it by an uncertainty margin are rejected. Wrapped around VINS-Fusion and DynaVINS on 8 self-collected sequences, referenced to FAST-LIO2. Median ATE RMSE e.g. E4 0.722 → 0.335 m, N3 0.845 → 0.460 m; slightly worse on N7 (highly specular floor) and E11 (dark corridor). Filter 4.93 ms/update; VINS-Fusion 15.0 → 14.7 Hz; floor segmenter 8.96 ms/image. Desktop i9 + RTX 4080 laptop GPU, not embedded.
- **Analysis:** a cheap, geometry-based gate that is front-end agnostic; the segmenter, not the filter, is the cost to budget on an embedded target.
- Loop closure / matching this week (abstracts only): UniLoop 2611.06378, scale anchoring for VGGT-based SLAM 2611.06308, CoReMatch 2611.03932, UAV on-demand features 2611.07687. See [Weekly 2026-11-09](../Weekly/2026-11-09.md), [Papers](../Research/Papers.md).

## Updates — 2026-11-13
- **StrataSLAM** (Universidad Rey Juan Carlos, open-source, ROS 2): multi-floor 3D SLAM with one submap per floor, a global map built from them, and detection and classification of floor transitions (stairs, ramps, elevators). Elevator rides are measured with the IMU when the camera or LiDAR sees only the cabin. A "floor gate" rejects every loop closure between different floors, so identical floors are never merged. [source fact, author post; no accuracy or runtime figures seen] https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/strataslam-open-source-multi-floor-slam-for-ros-2-stairs-elevators-ramps-identical-floors/59649
- **Analysis:** gating loop-closure candidates on a coarse discrete state (here, floor index) is a cheap guard against perceptual aliasing. It may also suit UAV VIO in multi-storey buildings, but that is an [hypothesis]; see the research opportunity in [Daily 2026-11-13](../Daily/2026-11-13.md).

## Updates — 2026-11-16
- **RTAB-Map vs ORB-SLAM3 on Raspberry Pi 5 (2611.16472, [measured] by authors, RGB-D, TUM sequences, no IMU):** ORB-SLAM3 tracking 13–45 ms/frame, RTAB-Map F2M odometry 38–74 ms/frame. End to end 26–62 ms against 150–462 ms, but RTAB-Map was configured to add a node and run its back end on every frame with unbounded working memory, a worst case rather than its 1 Hz default. In RTAB-Map the largest cost was pose-graph optimisation over the growing graph (GTSAM); in ORB-SLAM3 local bundle adjustment cost as much as tracking but ran in a parallel thread. The two systems failed on different sequences: ORB-SLAM3 never initialised on the low-texture fr3 sequences at reduced resolution, where RTAB-Map tracked one of them.
- **Benchmarking lesson from the same paper:** a run that loses tracking finishes sooner and scores lower ATE on the poses it keeps, so report tracking coverage with time and ATE. ATE varied by up to 27% between identical runs; single-run differences below about 40% are not evidence.
- **SLAM Dunk (UC Berkeley, [measured] by authors):** offline bundle adjustment with time-varying stereo calibration (5 s chunks, random walk) and a full 3×3 gyroscope intrinsic matrix. LaMAria training set: CP score 69.1 → 82.4, mean ATE 1.02 → 0.43 m; fitted focal length rose with IMU temperature on 22 of 23 and 23 of 23 recordings for the two cameras. Bundle adjustment took a median of 2 min per recording on 6 threads of a Ryzen 9 7950X3D; not causal, not real time.
- **NIF-VIO (2611.13662, [measured] by authors):** inside an accelerating carrier the camera sees carrier-relative motion while the IMU also senses the carrier. The method accumulates a log-likelihood ratio on the camera–IMU residual, withholds IMU factors at a low threshold, and on confirmation re-optimises the local map and switches to a constrained carrier-motion model. Correct-localisation rate above 98% on car, subway and elevator sequences; ORB-SLAM3 in VIO mode reached 24–77%.
- **Analysis:** all three point at modelling assumptions (fixed calibration, inertial world frame, unreported coverage) rather than front-end quality. None has been run on our hardware.
